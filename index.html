Meet your competitive sous-chef.

PokéChef is your AI-powered Pokémon assistant. Explore Pokémon and moves, understand type matchups, and import a Showdown team for practical feedback.

A sous-chef is a chef’s second-in-command. You build the team; PokéChef helps you refine the recipe.

We used a UI inspired by popular AI assistants like ChatGPT, with a Pokémon twist.

PokéChef is an independent project. AI advice can be wrong, and format legality is not verified. Pokémon artwork and sprites are provided through PokéAPI.
